Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Ductly doesn't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer has no server of its own. The only thing that leaves your devices is the optional shared timer described below, which contains no personal information.

What Ductly stores, and where

  • Your units' settings (names, Home and Away addresses, zone names and refresh interval) are stored on your device.
  • Each unit's PIN is stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Your Apple Watch receives the same settings and PINs directly from your iPhone using Apple's Watch Connectivity, so the Watch app can work.

Shared timers (Ductly Pro)

So that everyone who controls the same air conditioner can see when a timer will finish, Ductly Pro saves each timer's end time to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in a database shared by Ductly users. A saved timer contains only whether it turns the unit on or off, when it was set and when it ends. It's labelled with a one-way code made from the unit's serial number and PIN, so it can only be found by someone who already knows that PIN, and the serial number and PIN themselves are never uploaded. It isn't linked to your Apple Account or used for tracking. This needs you to be signed in to iCloud; without it, Ductly simply shows that a timer is set.

Deleting a unit in Ductly, or deleting the app, removes this information from your device.

Who Ductly talks to

Ductly only connects to the unit addresses that you enter. These are usually your unit on your home network, or a remote address that you set up yourself (such as dynamic DNS or a reverse proxy). That traffic goes directly between your device and your air conditioner.

These units only support plain HTTP, so traffic to an Away address on the internet, including the unit's PIN, isn't encrypted unless you put HTTPS in front of it with your own reverse proxy. Ductly warns you about this when you enter such an address.

Purchases

Ductly Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer doesn't receive your payment details. Ductly only checks with the App Store whether Pro has been purchased.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports. These don't identify you.

Children

Ductly doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes
