Ductly

Ducted air conditioning and zone control for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Ductly controls a ducted air conditioner with a compatible Wi‑Fi controller from your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch: power, mode, fan, temperature, zones and timers. It talks directly to your unit, with no account and no cloud.

Compatibility

Ductly works with ducted air conditioners fitted with a compatible local Wi‑Fi controller: one that you reach on your home network with its IP address and a PIN, with no online account.

  • How to tell: a compatible unit has a label with its PIN, and its wall controller has ▲/▼ TEMP, MODE, FAN and ON/OFF TIMER keys above a row of zone buttons.
  • Not supported: Wi‑Fi adapters that only work through a cloud service or online account.
  • Not sure? Ductly is free. Install it, tap Add Your Unit, enter its address and PIN, and tap Test connection. If it connects, it's compatible.

What you need

  • A ducted air conditioner with a compatible Wi‑Fi controller, connected to your home Wi‑Fi.
  • The unit's IP address on your network, for example 192.168.1.50. Your router's list of connected devices shows it.
  • The unit's PIN, printed on the unit's label.

Tip: reserve the unit's IP address in your router (often called a DHCP reservation) so it doesn't change.

Common questions

Ductly can't connect at home

Check that your device is on the same Wi‑Fi as the unit, and that Ductly is allowed Local Network access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Then check the unit's Home address in Ductly's Settings (tap ⓘ next to the unit).

Can I control it away from home?

Yes, if you set up remote access yourself: for example dynamic DNS with port forwarding on your router, or a reverse proxy. Enter that address as the unit's Away address. Ductly tries Home first, then Away. These units only speak plain HTTP, so HTTPS on your reverse proxy is recommended.

The unit is slow to respond

These units can only handle one request at a time. Ductly sends requests one by one and retries automatically. A longer refresh interval in Settings can help.

What does Ductly Pro include?

A one-time purchase (not a subscription), shared with your Family Sharing group, that adds:

  • more than one unit
  • the Apple Watch app and complications
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Control Centre and Lock Screen controls
  • Siri and Shortcuts

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings → Ductly Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I try it without a unit?

Yes. Add the Demo Unit from the welcome screen or Settings. It behaves like a real unit but doesn't control anything.
